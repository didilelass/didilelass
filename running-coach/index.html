The rules

  • Keep at least one easy day between hard runs (intervals and tempo).
  • No leg day the day before intervals or tempo.
  • Most of your running should feel easy. That's what lets the hard days work.
  • Suggested week: Tue intervals · Wed easy · Thu rest · Fri tempo · Sun long run · optional easy on Mon or Sat.

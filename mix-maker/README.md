# 🎧 Mix Maker

Pick the songs, and the app makes the DJ mix: it matches the beats, blends the songs and gives you an MP3, a YouTube tracklist and a cover image. Made for Afrobeats × Hip Hop mixes, and works on an iPhone.

**Open it:** https://didilelass.github.io/didilelass/mix-maker/

## How to use it
1. **Add songs.** For 30 minutes you need about 10–14 MP3, M4A or WAV files.
2. **Tap ✨ Auto-order.** The app orders the songs so the tempos and keys flow and the energy builds. Use ↑ ↓ to change anything your ear disagrees with.
3. **Check the transitions.** Tap **▶ Hear** to listen to one. Tap the transition to change it: Auto → Blend → Echo out → Cut.
4. **Tap 🎧 Make my mix** and keep the screen open. A 30 minute mix takes a few minutes on a phone.
5. **Download** the MP3 and the cover image, and copy the tracklist.

## What the app does for you
- **Finds the BPM and key** of every song (Camelot keys like 8A, 9B).
- **Blend (4 bars)** for songs with similar tempos: speeds the new song up or down to lock the beats, brings it in on the downbeat, then swaps the bass halfway so the two kicks don't clash.
- **Echo out** for big tempo jumps, like hip hop (~90 BPM) into Afrobeats (~105): the old song echoes away and the new one drops in on the beat.
- **Evens out the volume** so no song is much louder than the others.
- **Tracklist with timestamps**, so YouTube shows chapters.

## Put it on YouTube
YouTube needs a video. In **iMovie** or **CapCut** (both free), add the cover image, stretch it to the length of the song, add the MP3 and export. Paste the tracklist in the description.

⚠️ Commercial songs usually get a Content ID claim: the video stays up but the labels get the ad money. See "Copyright" inside the app.

Everything runs on your device. Your songs are never uploaded. MP3 encoding by [LAME](https://lame.sourceforge.io) via lamejs (LGPL, in `vendor/`).

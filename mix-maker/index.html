    How to put it on YouTube

    YouTube needs a video, so you combine the MP3 with the cover image:

    1. iPhone, iMovie (free): new Movie → add the cover image → tap the image and stretch it to the full length → add the MP3 as audio → Share → Save Video.
    2. CapCut (free, iPhone and Android): New project → add the cover image → add the MP3 under Audio → drag the image end to match the music → Export at 1080p.
    3. Upload. Paste the tracklist into the description and use the cover as the thumbnail.
    Copyright: read before you upload
    • Mixes made of commercial songs usually get a Content ID claim on YouTube. Most often the video stays up but the labels get the ad money. Some videos are blocked in some countries or muted.
    • A claim is not a strike. Strikes only come from takedown requests, which are rarer for mixes.
    • For a mix channel you can earn from, use royalty-free or Creative Commons music, or songs whose artists say mixes are allowed.
    • Mixcloud pays licences for DJ mixes. Many DJs upload the full mix there and post clips or links on YouTube.
    Tips for a better mix
    • The BPM tag is the song's speed. Songs within about 6% of each other get a smooth 4-bar Blend, with the beats matched.
    • Bigger jumps, like hip hop (≈90) to Afrobeats (≈105), get an Echo out: the old song echoes away and the new one drops in. Group your hip hop and your Afrobeats and you'll mostly get smooth blends.
    • The key tag (like 8A) shows which songs sound good together. Numbers next to each other (8A → 9A, or 8A → 8B) blend best.
    • Tap a transition to change it: Auto → Blend → Echo → Cut. Tap ▶ Hear to listen to just that transition.
    • If a song has a long talking intro, type a start time (like 0:24) so it comes in where the beat starts.
